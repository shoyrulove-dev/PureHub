# PureHub 1.0.0-beta.55

PureHub beta.55 expands Lunar Calendar and connects Pure Earn to the Community area without merging accounts or sharing device identifiers.

## Lunar Calendar

- Vietnam UTC+7 and China UTC+8 regional calculation modes.
- Solar-to-lunar and lunar-to-solar conversion with leap-month handling.
- Personal reminders with selectable time, multiple lead times, monthly/yearly recurrence, categories, enable/disable, countdown, trash, and restore.
- Personal-event markers in the month calendar and quick reminder creation from day details.
- Today, month, and upcoming-date Android widgets.
- ICS, PNG, PDF, and encrypted local backup/restore workflows.
- Offline moon illumination, age, rise/set, altitude, azimuth, and next new/full moon estimates.
- Calculation-method screen and expanded 1900–2100 regional round-trip tests.

## Community and Pure Earn

- New official Pure Earn banner in Android and PWA Community pages.
- Opens `earn.blissbiovn.com` as a separate external service with campaign attribution.
- PureHub does not pass Android ID, email, account tokens, or stored app data to Pure Earn.
- Community remains available in English, Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese, and Spanish.

## Verification

- 70 Android unit tests passed on the release candidate.
- Physical-device debug verification completed on an ARM64 Android phone.
- PWA localization, lint, production build, service-worker generation, and prerender validation passed.
