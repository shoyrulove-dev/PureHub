# PureHub Code of Conduct

PureHub should be a welcoming place for users, testers, translators, designers, and developers of different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and implementation without attacking people.
- Assume good intent while accepting corrections.
- Protect private information shared during support or testing.
- Give credit for contributions and respect licenses.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam, deliberate disruption, credential sharing, and publishing another person's sensitive data are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Serious security or safety concerns should be reported privately through the contact path in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, impact, and whether behavior is repeated. Retaliation against a good-faith report is not permitted.
