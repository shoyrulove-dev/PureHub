# PureHub Flagship and Live Support Upgrade — 2026-08-10

## Outcome

PureHub now has 12 normalized flagship mini-apps and a Live Support Inbox that refreshes recent conversations without requiring a manual page reload. The flagship pass keeps all processing local where the browser or Android platform supports it, with no ads and no account requirement for public tools.

## Seven delivered priorities

1. **Live Support Inbox** — five-minute background sync, live status, safe auto-refresh, conversation counts, and clear `Awaiting us`, `Awaiting user`, `Reopened`, and `Resolved` states.
2. **Flagship metadata** — all 12 flagship IDs are normalized to flagship priority 10 by schema migration 17.
3. **OCR Studio** — visible language-pack download progress and local readiness labels for previously cached OCR languages.
4. **Document Suite** — drag-to-reorder pages, per-page edge crop, rotation, and an estimated PDF size before export.
5. **Finance Suite** — equal, weighted, and item-by-item splitting; payer selection; tax/tip settlement guidance; local budget, currency, trends, and CSV export.
6. **Sensor Suite** — permission guidance, safe listener cleanup, Compass and Level calibration, hold/resume controls, and Sound Meter minimum/average/peak history.
7. **Speaker Cleaner** — before/after listening workflow on web, clearer Android completion state, timed presets, safe-stop guidance, and no recording.
